Privacy Policy

Daily Finds

Who we are

Daily Finds runs a small set of Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts that share product videos and recommendations. We use a private publishing tool, connected to the Meta Graph API, to schedule posts and comments on accounts we own or manage.

What data we access

The tool only accesses data of the Facebook Pages and Instagram business accounts that belong to our own Business Manager: account names and IDs, our own posts and comments, and basic engagement numbers. We do not collect, store or sell personal data of people who view or interact with our content.

How we use it

Data is used only to publish our own content, add comments with product links, and check that posts were published. It is stored on our own computer and never shared with third parties.

Affiliate links

Your rights

You can ask us about any data related to you, or ask us to delete it, at any time. See Data deletion.
